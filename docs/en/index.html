KUT

Your personal network, only on your computer.

See the people you know on a map with their photos. Jot one line after every conversation. Let Kut remember who you have been neglecting, what you promised to whom, and who you should write to this week. No account, no cloud: your data stays on your disk as plain Markdown files.

Features

A calm way to keep up with the people in your life

Professors, friends, mentors, colleagues: all on one map, each in their own file. You write, Kut reminds.

Network map

You in the center, the people you know around you

Color shows the type of contact, the ring shows how close they are. Links are drawn as friendship, mentorship, business partnership, family, rivalry or tension. The Watch out list flags possible conflicts of interest from those links.

academia work close circle events finance politics

Contact card and contact rhythm

Kut tells you who you are neglecting

After a conversation you add a one-line contact note. Kut looks at the last contact and closeness and works out a neglect threshold:

closeness 3
14 days closeness 2
30 days closeness 1
60 days

Six relationship scores are computed from your records: warmth, depth, reciprocity, continuity, bridging and common ground. The “How are scores calculated?” panel shows the exact criteria.

Promises

Promises you made, and were made to you, are not forgotten

“I'll send the book”, “she'll forward the internship posting”. Each promise sits on the contact's card with its direction and due date. Just type a plain sentence into Ask Kut:

KutAdd this as a promise to Selin? (due: 16.10)

Nothing is written until you confirm. The promise stays in the contact file as a readable line (the file format is Turkish):

- [ ] 2026-10-13 · ben → o · kitabı göndermek (son: 2026-10-17)

This week and Compass

Who to write to, and why now

The This week list brings together steps that are due, relationships going cold and overdue promises; one click highlights only them on the map.

You write your goals into Compass (Pusula). If you want, an AI reads your goals and your network and suggests moves, each tied to its evidence: who, why now, and what the first step is.

DoneNot doneWorkedDid not workInvalid / wrong

The assistant asks

The AI's inferences about people are never written to a file until you approve them. For each one you say Right, Fix or Wrong; what you say always wins.

Import

WhatsApp chats, contacts (.vcf), calendar (.ics), LinkedIn export (CSV) and course lists. You see a preview first; it is written only if you approve. Message content is not kept: only who, when and which channel.

Ask Kut

Splits a plain sentence like “Talked to Professor Selin today, my application was approved” into a contact note, a statement and a next step. Before every write, the previous version of the contact is copied to an archive.

Local voice

Take notes by speaking if you like: Whisper transcribes, EMA Lightning reads answers aloud (Turkish voice). Everything runs on this computer, and audio is never written to disk.

School adapter

Write “I'm taking ISE 424 and MATH 101 this term”; if your school is selected, Kut fills in instructors and class times from public pages, with sources. Off when no school is selected; adapters currently cover Turkish universities.

PIN lock

Kut asks for a PIN at startup. The lock is server-side: while locked, no personal data is served. Kut locks itself after a period of inactivity.

Try a demo first

Deniz's Tuesday

Before entering your own data, press Önce bir örnekle göster (show me an example first) in the first-run wizard. Six scenes play out on a fictional network: Compass spotting a relationship gone cold, a promise being captured, a relationship warming up again.

The demo runs in a separate temporary folder, never touches your data and makes no network or model calls. It is deleted when you close it.

Privacy

Your data on your disk, in a format you can read

Every contact is a Markdown file. Open it in any editor, back it up, delete it. Kut runs a server, but it only listens on your own computer at 127.0.0.1.

  • No account, no cloud, no telemetry.No sync and no mobile app either. The server stops by itself about 20 minutes after you close the page.
  • No internet access unless you turn it on.On a fresh install no external source is enabled. You enable each one yourself in Settings → Sources.
  • AI is optional.Claude Code (recommended), Codex, Gemini or local Ollama. Kut never asks for or stores an API key; everything works without AI too.
  • Every record sent to a model passes a local gate first.A contact you mark “never send to AI” never leaves. Keys, tokens and passwords are masked before anything is sent.

Install

Pick one of three ways, a few minutes

On first run you set a PIN; then a short wizard asks for your name and AI choice. Every step can be skipped.

WINDOWS · KUT.EXE

No Python needed

  1. Download Kut.exe from GitHub Releases and double-click it.
  2. If Windows says “unknown publisher”: More info → Run anyway. The file is unsigned; you can verify it with the Kut.exe.sha256 next to it.
  3. Your data lives in Documents\Kut.
Go to latest release
PYTHON 3 · ANY OS

Run from source

Standard library only; nothing to install.

git clone https://github.com/koraykutlu/kut
py -3 kut.pyw

On macOS and Linux: python3 kut.pyw. Your browser opens at 127.0.0.1:47318.

WITH AN AI AGENT

Let your agent install it

If you use Claude Code, Codex or a similar agent, just tell it:

Install Kut from https://github.com/koraykutlu/kut

The agent follows the install instructions in the repo, keeps your personal data in a folder outside the repo, and never enables external sources without your approval.

Updates happen inside the app: a new version is not installed until its SHA-256 is verified, the previous version is kept for rollback, and your data folder is never touched.
